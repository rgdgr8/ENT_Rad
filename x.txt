Do not use “first usable identity found” unless your search order is deterministic and ranked by reliability.

Otherwise you could accidentally select a stale mediaId while a better DAB SID is available.

Recommended strategy

Radio application's exposed media data
                │
                ▼
     Identify CURRENT media/station
                │
                ▼
 Search its identity-bearing fields
                │
       ┌────────┼──────────┐
       ▼        ▼          ▼
    Bearer     SID      DAB identifiers
       │        │
       └────┬───┘
            │
       preferred identity
            │
            ▼
         mediaId
        (fallback)
            │
            ▼
   Active station identity
            │
            ▼
 Correlate with MediaBrowser item
            │
            ▼
           RPUID
            │
            ▼
          RPSDK
